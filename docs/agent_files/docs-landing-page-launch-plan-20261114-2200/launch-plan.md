14 November 2026. Jira: [ONOLYS-72](https://onolys.atlassian.net/browse/ONOLYS-72). Research behind this plan: [Onolys MI evidence check: competitors, buyers and channels 2026-11-14](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tfX5dWq30E9-kgt9W_ppEQBE7W7uM0R1J2xRsQ6n0O4/edit). Labels: DECISION (agreed by Tejit on 14 Nov), FACT (from the research doc, sources there), HYPOTHESIS (not tested), PROPOSAL (suggested, not agreed).

## **Decision**

  - DECISION: we market a **full MI evaluation**: upload a session transcript and get a complete MITI 4.2.1 evaluation (global ratings, behaviour counts, summary scores and written feedback), with every score linked to the moment in the session.
  - DECISION: the audience is **learners and independent practitioners**: people learning MI, and people who today send their own work samples to a coder to be evaluated.
  - DECISION: price for learners is **$5** (assumed per evaluation).
  - DECISION: a landing page with email sign-up.
  - DECISION: in parallel, contact **MITI coding services** and offer the evidence check (quality control of their coders' work) as a tool that makes their coders faster and more consistent, at a lower rate than a second human coder. Not pitched as a replacement for their service.
  - Tejit builds the product the week of 16 November.

## **One engine, two offers**

The engine takes a protocol (MITI 4.2.1 first), a transcript and, optionally, an existing evaluation. It returns protocol findings and evidence links.

|  |  |  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
|   | Full evaluation | Evidence check |
| Who | Learners, independent practitioners | Coding services, trainers, supervisors |
| Input | Transcript and change goal | Transcript and a human MITI coding sheet or feedback |
| Output | MITI 4.2.1 globals, behaviour counts, summary scores, written feedback; each linked to transcript lines | Each human score and comment marked supported, partly supported, not supported or not enough evidence, with lines |
| Price | $5 per evaluation | PROPOSAL: $5 to $10 per checked recording, first 10 free |
| Marketed through | Landing page, communities | Direct email |

Neither offer is official MITI coding or certification. The person reading the result decides what to do with it.

## **Why this offer**

  - FACT: human MITI coding costs $40 (HETI) to $80 (C4) per sample, with 7 to 10 business days turnaround. $5 in minutes is a large difference for learners who want feedback often.
  - FACT: AI practice tools (MI Mastery about $9.99 a month, MI Practice Space $10 a month, MIRROR about $29.99 a month) give "MITI-informed" feedback on chats with a simulated client. They do not evaluate a session the learner uploads.
  - FACT: Tagaroo codes behaviour counts with evidence but gives no global ratings. Lyssn gives full MITI 4.2.1 but sells only to organisations.
  - HYPOTHESIS: a full MITI evaluation of your own session, with every score linked to the lines, at $5, is not offered by anyone found.

## **Track A: landing page and sign-up**

### **Who it is for**

  - **Learners:** students and practitioners in social work, counselling, nursing, medicine, pharmacy and addiction services who practise MI with role-plays or real sessions and want regular feedback.
  - **Independent practitioners sending work samples:** people who pay a coder today to evaluate a sample, for a course, a supervisor or a MINT trainer application. Our evaluation lets them practise and check before they pay for the official one.

### **What we ask for**

  - Email sign-up, with role and how often they would want an evaluation.
  - "Would you pay $5 per evaluation?" (yes / maybe / no).
  - Stronger signal: "Send a role-play or de-identified transcript for a free first evaluation" (first 10 to 20 people).
  - OPTION: a $5 pre-order through a payment link. Strongest signal of the three; decide before launch.

### **How we judge it (PROPOSAL)**

  - Launch Monday 16 November. Judge Monday 30 November, or after 300 visitors, whichever is later.
  - Pass: 5% or more of visitors sign up, and at least half of those say yes or maybe to $5; or 5 or more people send a transcript; or any paid pre-orders if we use them.
  - Stop or rethink: under 2% sign-up after 300 visitors.
  - Track each channel with its own link tag.

## **Track B: coding-service outreach**

### **Targets**

  - HETI (Maine): $40 per coded recording, $80 with a simulated client.
  - C4 Coding and Coaching Lab: $80 per 20-minute sample.
  - ASU SIRC MI Portal: coders run ongoing reliability checks.
  - UNC CFAR: about $95 per hour of coding.
  - MINT-approved coding labs that score trainer applications.

### **Offer (PROPOSAL)**

  - An evidence check of each coded sample before it goes back to the client: flags scores the transcript does not support, with line references.
  - Price to test: $5 to $10 per checked recording, first 10 free. Basis (HYPOTHESIS): 10 minutes of coder time saved at $60 an hour is worth $10.
  - Ask: one 20-minute call, then 5 to 10 already-coded samples (role-play or with client permission) for a free check.

### **Tension between the tracks**

  - A $5 full evaluation competes with these services for learners who want feedback. HYPOTHESIS: the overlap is small if we position our evaluation as practice and a check before an official submission, and their coding as the official, human result. Keep the two offers on separate pages and messages.

## **Risks**

  - **Accuracy of AI scores.** FACT: in MI-TAGS, GPT-4 global ratings agreed with experts at 0.34 to 0.63; complex reflections are hard for models. Handle: every score shows its evidence lines, we state that it is not official MITI coding, and we measure agreement before publishing any number. Idea 9 in the next-steps doc proposes paying trained coders to double-code about 30 recordings ($2,500 to $6,000).
  - **Wrong use for official submissions.** People may try to submit our evaluation as their MITI coding. The page says it is for practice and preparation only.
  - **Upsetting MI trainers and coders.** Many MINT members sell coding. Messages to them should present the tool as practice support for their learners, not a cheaper substitute.
  - **Privacy.** Role-play or de-identified transcripts only, no training on uploaded data, stated deletion period.
  - **Unit cost at $5.** HYPOTHESIS: model cost for a 20-minute transcript is well under $5; audio transcription adds cost. Check during the build.
  - **Demo data licence.** MI-TAGS and AnnoMI licences are not confirmed. Use a role-play we write and record for the public example.
